Identities (cheat sheet)

PREDICTED until STAND. Cited NTRS bounds only — not invented station numbers. η from CFD: forbidden.

  • density (ρ) = pressure (p) / (specific gas constant (R) × temperature (T)) by default. Edit ρ to hold it; residual |p − ρ R T| / p while held.
  • sound speed (a) = √(γ R T); relative inlet Mach (Mw1) = |W1| / a. TN D-4422 published sweep 1.5–5.0, NTRS 19680010807. 1.0 ≤ Mw1 < 1.5: method valid, off the 1968 chart (INFO, not NOMINAL). Do not read thickness/solidity off those plots.
  • Metal knobs (mm): hu, hl, r_LE, r_TE, L_in, L_out, t, R_tr, R_main, ψ_tr. β1/β2 stagger/heading. Mw1 = W1/a derived, display-only. ML/MU are not metal.
  • blade speed (U) = ω rm = 2π N/60 · rm. N shaft rpm, rm mean radius. PREDICTED identity, not an NTRS nominal.
  • Euler Δh0 = U (Cθ1 − Cθ2); power ṁ Δh0 PREDICTED meanline.
  • OF power per blade = Ft · U after force_plateau only; not η.
  • Zweifel (Zw) = 2 (s / cx) cos²(β2) (tan β1 − tan β2) SP-290. NOMINAL 0.8, NASA-SP-290 / NTRS 19740025363. s pitch, cx axial chord, β from axial.
  • turning |β1 − β2|: NOMINAL 140 deg, NTRS 19940018586; context 160 deg NTRS 19920066198.
  • tend ≥ max(5 c/W1, 1.2 Lx/a). c chord, Lx domain length, a sound speed.
  • outlet p: waveTransmissive, fieldInf = p1, not 0.95 p1.
  • η from CFD: forbidden.
